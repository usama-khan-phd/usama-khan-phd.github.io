Welcome 👋  I build data-driven and AI-powered tools that make construction smarter, safer, and faster.

I am a PhD researcher working at the intersection of artificial intelligence, human factors engineering, and civil & construction engineering. My research develops practical decision-support and automation systems for the built environment — spanning machine learning, digital twins, and predictive modeling.

A central thread of my work is a cross-sector framework for human-factor technologies, examining how tools built for vertical construction (buildings) transfer to horizontal construction (transportation and infrastructure), and vice versa. Alongside research, I design software that replaces slow, manual engineering workflows with automated, data-driven pipelines.

🎯 Currently open to Summer 2026 opportunities in AI for construction, construction technology, and applied data science. If our interests overlap, I'd love to connect.

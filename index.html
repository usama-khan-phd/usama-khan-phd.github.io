I automate engineering tasks and develop custom software solutions for construction.

For more than 10 years I have built custom software, automation, and engineering solutions for construction. As a planning engineer and project manager, I delivered project control systems, automated reporting, and live dashboards for active megaprojects across the UAE and Pakistan.

I am currently a Graduate Research Assistant at Texas State University, pursuing a PhD in Materials Science, Engineering and Commercialization. My research focuses on human factors and human AI interaction in construction, and I am developing new monitoring technology to improve worker health and safety on the job site. This doctoral work positions me to turn research into deployable, market ready products.
